Recruiter problems

Quick ideas for common issues – you can later add your own playbook here.

  • Low response on outreach: shorten message, add one clear question, try different timing.
  • No-shows on interview: confirm twice (day before + 2 hours before) with clear agenda.
  • Hiring manager slow feedback: send 3-line summary + ask for specific yes/no on shortlist.
  • Too many rejections after submission: align on 3 must-have points with examples in resume.
